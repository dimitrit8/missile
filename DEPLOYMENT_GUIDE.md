# DEPLOYMENT GUIDE - Missile Tracking Dashboard

## What Happens When You Deploy?

### 1. YOU GET A PUBLIC WEBSITE 🌐

When you click "Deploy" in Emergent:
- Your app becomes a **live, public website**
- You get a **permanent URL** like: `https://missile-tracking-dashboard.emergent.app`
- **Anyone in the world** can access it with that link
- It's **hosted 24/7** on professional servers
- **No installation needed** - works in any web browser

### 2. How to Share It

After deployment, you can share your dashboard by:

**Direct Link:**
- Copy the URL and send it via email, WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.
- Post it on social media (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook)
- Add it to your website or blog

**Embed Options:**
- Embed the dashboard in your website using an iframe
- Share it in presentations or reports

**QR Code:**
- Generate a QR code for the URL
- Print it on posters, business cards, or documents
